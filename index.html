Virtual SEM

Every control acts through the physics from the journey: the aberration budget sets the blur, the dose sets the shot noise, yield curves set the contrast and the charge balance decides whether an insulator behaves.

Model: SE yield from the Lin–Joy universal curve with a sec θ tilt law and BSE-generated SE2 delocalised over the backscatter escape radius; BSE coefficient from Reuter’s fit; probe diameter from source, spherical, chromatic and diffraction terms in quadrature; Poisson noise from electrons per pixel. Specimens are procedural. It is a teaching model, not a metrology simulator.
